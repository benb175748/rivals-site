Privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Rivals is a mobile app for challenging friends to skill games. This policy explains what we collect when you use it, why, who helps us run it, and how to have your data deleted. Rivals is run by Ben Burgess ("we"). Questions: benburgess1234@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address, and a password if you sign up with email (stored hashed by our sign-in provider, never readable by us).
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google: the email address and name they share with us. With Apple you can choose to hide your email; we then get a private relay address.

Your profile

  • Your handle and display name, which other players can see.
  • Your date of birth, to confirm you are 18 or older. It is not shown to other players.

Play

  • Groups you create or join, challenges, your moves and inputs in each match, scores, results, ratings and ranks, reactions and posts in your groups, and your coin balance and history. Your opponents and group members see results and activity from games you play with them.

Approximate location

  • If you allow location access, we record your country and state or region on each challenge, for local leaderboards and to know where features are allowed. We do not store your exact location.

Device and network

  • A hashed device identifier and a hashed IP address, to keep play fair (for example, stopping one person from playing both sides of a match). We store only the hashes, not the raw values.
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications, so we can tell you when a friend challenges you or it's your turn.

Interest in new features

  • When you tap Cash, view the Cash screen or ask to be notified, we record that, so we can tell how many people want it. Cash play is not available in the app.

Ads

Rivals may show ads, for example an ad you choose to watch for a coin refill. Ads are provided by advertising partners such as Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, these partners may collect information from your device, such as its advertising identifier, IP address, device type and how you interact with the ad.

  • Personalized ads are only shown if you allow it. On iPhone, the app asks "Allow tracking?" first; if you say no, you still get ads, but not ones based on your activity in other apps and websites. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • In the EU, UK and similar places, you'll be asked for consent before ads use your data.
  • We don't give advertisers your name, email, date of birth or games.

Our ad partners handle the data they collect under their own privacy policies, for example Google's.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We don't collect your contacts, photos or exact location.

Who helps us run Rivals

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and servers (United States).
  • Apple and Google handle Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google when you choose them.
  • Expo delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Advertising partners such as Google AdMob, if ads are shown (see Ads above).
  • Apple TestFlight distributes test versions of the app and may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send.

They process data only to provide these services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and play history while your account exists, so your results, ratings and group history stay accurate.

When you delete your account, right away: your sign-in (email, password, Apple or Google link), name, handle, date of birth, push tokens, device records, ratings and ranks are deleted, and you leave all your groups. Your past matches stay in your opponents' history under "Deleted player", so their results and coin totals still add up. Match records keep one-way hashes of the device and network used, for fair-play checks; these can't be turned back into your device or IP address and aren't linked to anything that identifies you. We may keep records we are required to keep by law.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Profile > Delete account. It takes effect immediately. Open challenges are cancelled with stakes refunded, and a match you're in the middle of goes to your opponent. If you can't sign in any more, email us and we'll delete it for you.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we'll send you a copy or fix it.
  • Location and notifications: you can turn these off any time in your iPhone's Settings. The app still works without them.

Age

Rivals is for people 18 and older. If we learn an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules in our database, so players can only read what the app shows them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
