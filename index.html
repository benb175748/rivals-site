How it works

Same game, same conditions

Both players get the same course, rack, wind or word grid, so the better player wins. No luck, no pay-to-win.

Play your friends

Start a group, invite friends with a code, and send challenges. Results, streaks and the group's ledger keep score.

Free coins

Everyone starts with 1,000 coins. They refill, and they can't be bought or cashed out. Rivals is for players 18 and over.
